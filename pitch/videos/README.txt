LOOP VIDEOS — Quick Style Pitch Studio

1. Drop videos in this folder (MP4 / H.264 works everywhere; .mov and .webm also accepted).
2. Double-click "Update video list.command".
3. In Pitch Studio → open a pitch → "Videos & photos" tab → tick them under Loop videos.
   Ticked videos autoplay on the pitch, muted and looping. #1 is shown large, the rest below.

Tips
- Keep each clip short (5–20 s) and under ~15 MB so pitches load fast on phones.
- Don't rename or delete a video that a live pitch uses; the backend flags missing ones in red.
- When you publish the site, upload this whole folder including videos.json.
